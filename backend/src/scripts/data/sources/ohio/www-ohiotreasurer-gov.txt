Mission Statement NEW FROM THE OHIO TREASURER'S OFFICE Robert Sprague currently serves as Ohio’s 49th Treasurer of State. As Treasurer, he is the chief investment officer for the state’s investment and debt portfolios, oversees the daily cash flows of state monies, and serves as custodian of certain funds and investment assets outside the state treasury.
